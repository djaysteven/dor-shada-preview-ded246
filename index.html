About us

A green Na Jomtien retreat

Nestled in lush greenery, Dor-Shada Resort offers 5-star accommodations with cable flat-screen TVs and complimentary WiFi. Located in Na Jomtien, with an outdoor pool with a poolside bar, and a full-service spa.

The modern Thai-style rooms come with private balconies and spacious seating areas with sofas. Each room is equipped with a safe, refrigerator, and a private bathroom that includes a bathtub and hairdryer.

Guests can stay active at the well-equipped fitness center. The resort also provides a tour desk and free on-site parking.

Enjoy Thai and international cuisine with sweeping sea views at the beachfront restaurant, or arrange a barbecue party. Room service is also available for added convenience.
